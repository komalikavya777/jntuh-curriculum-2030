Where this differs from R25

All comparisons are against the constituent-colleges R25 CSE structure, which is the document governing my college — not the affiliated-colleges version, which differs on DevOps.

Machine Learning placement
R25 moves ML to II-II, the same semester as its own statistics prerequisite. I keep it in III-II, three semesters after COSM.
differ
DevOps
R25 constituent cuts 4 credits to a 1-credit skill course. I keep all 4 and add Cloud & Distributed Systems after it. JNTUH's own affiliated-colleges R25 keeps DevOps at 3 + lab.
differ
Generative AI
R25 makes it a Professional Elective II option plus a 1-credit Prompt Engineering practical. I make it a 3 + 1 core and drop the standalone prompting credit.
differ
NLP and Cyber Security as separate cores
R25 adds both in IV-I, 3 + 1 credits each. I fold NLP into Generative AI Systems and rewrite CS701PC's content instead, spending those 8 credits on Cloud and on Verification.
differ
