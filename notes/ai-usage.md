# Notes on AI usage

AI (Claude) was used throughout this project for:

- Reading and summarising the R22 and R25 syllabus PDFs to build the Section 2 course tables
- Drafting initial Section 3 KEEP/UPDATE/REDUCE/ADD arguments, which were then corrected and
  refined based on my own judgement (e.g. DevOps was corrected from REDUCE to KEEP — see Section 7b)
- Running the actual (a)/(b) tests in Section 3: acting as "the model" against my real lab sheets
  for Compiler Design, FLAT, Data Structures, and DBMS, then being pushed further to find edge
  cases and failures
- Helping structure and word Sections 4-7 from findings I had already gathered
- General Git/GitHub troubleshooting during setup

AI output was never treated as evidence on its own — every factual claim was checked against
the actual R22/R25 PDFs, and one specific case of AI generating a false claim (the fabricated
"Web Technologies" and "Full Stack Development Lab" core subjects) is documented and corrected
in Section 3.
